Our Mission

We Don't Just Train Bodies.
We Build Champions.

Founded in 2009 by former Olympic athletes Jordan Mercer and Aisha Cole, APEX GYM started as a single 3,000 sq ft space with a vision: to bring elite-level training to everyday people without compromise.

Today, with over 5,000 active members and a team of 32 certified professionals, APEX stands as the region's most trusted performance fitness facility — and we're just getting started.

Our Journey

A Decade of Impact

From a single training floor to a full-service performance hub, every milestone reflects our obsession with building something extraordinary for our community.

2009

APEX Is Born

Opened our first 3,000 sq ft location with 8 staff members and a bold vision.

2012

Expansion & Growth

Moved to a 12,000 sq ft facility, tripling membership capacity and adding group classes.

2016

Performance Hub Launch

Introduced the Recovery Zone, sports nutrition bar, and biomechanical analysis studio.

2020

Digital Integration

Launched APEX App with virtual coaching, workout tracking, and live-streamed classes.

2024

5,000 Members Strong

Reached a landmark milestone with a fully renovated 20,000 sq ft state-of-the-art facility.
